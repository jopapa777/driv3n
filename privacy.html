Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

DRIV3N ("the app") is made by James Burroughs. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Everything you enter stays on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share your data, we do not use ads or tracking, and the app has no account or sign-in.

Information stored on your device

  • Jobs, miles, expenses, goals, and settings you enter are saved only on your iPhone.
  • Location: only while a trip you started is running, to record your route and miles. You can deny or change this any time in iOS Settings. The rest of the app works without it.
  • Motion activity: used during a trip to pause GPS while you are parked, to save battery.
  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to take or pick a receipt photo for an expense.
  • Face ID: if you turn on App Lock, iOS checks your face or passcode. The app never receives your face data.

Sharing

Your data leaves your device only when you choose to export or share it (for example a CSV or report through the iOS share sheet). Device backups are handled by Apple under your iCloud settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
