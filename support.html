Support

Questions, problems, or ideas? Email support@37driv3n.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

Trip tracking is not recording miles. Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → DRIV3N and choose “While Using the App” or “Always”.

How do I add the widget? Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for DRIV3N.

How do I back up my data? In the app go to Settings → Data and export a CSV file, then save it somewhere safe.

I forgot to unlock / Face ID failed. Tap Unlock again or use your device passcode.

In the app, Settings → Help & Guide explains every screen.
